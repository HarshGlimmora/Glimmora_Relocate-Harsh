Personas×Portals×Roles.

Three different questions, three different counts. This document shows — in one view — which persona uses which portal and holds which role, so the numbers never trip anyone again.

Personas   9 Portals   6 (+1 optional) Human roles   22 System accounts   2

01 — Three dimensionsWhat each count is measuring.

Personas, portals, and roles answer three different questions. Confusing them is the single most common source of mis-communication on this project.

Persona
9

Who shows up?

A kind of real-world person with motivations and constraints. A product concept. Used for thinking about “who would want this?” Mover, Family Unit, Student, Employer, Partner, HR Admin, Ops, Curator, Verified Expert.

Portal
6

Where do they log in?

A software surface — a web or mobile app. A product / design concept. Consumer App, Employer Portal, Partner Portal, Corporate Portal, Admin Console, Public Site. (An optional 7th Expert Portal ships with the premium tier.)

Role
22

What can they do once logged in?

A named set of permissions inside the software. An engineering concept. Used for “who is allowed to call this API, see this screen, approve this action?” Plus 2 non-human system accounts (Copilot, Integration).

Why the counts differ. A single portal can serve multiple personas (three personas share the Consumer App). A single persona can hold multiple roles (an Employer company has an Owner, Recruiters, and Hiring Managers — all one persona, five roles). A single role can be used by multiple personas (the Ops and Curator personas both work inside the Admin Console, but with different role codes). The three dimensions are independent on purpose.

05 — Complete role indexAll 22 human roles, plus 2 system accounts.

Every role in one list, with what it means. Use this when modelling permissions or writing auth code.

Consumer App

3 roles
Primary User consumer.primary
Full owner of a relocation plan. Subscribes, books, pays, manages documents.
Linked Family Member consumer.family
Spouse or adult child invited by a Primary. Own login and twin; shared view of the plan.
Student consumer.student
Primary in student mode — narrower Copilot, different plan templates.

Employer Portal

5 roles
Employer Owner employer.owner
Creates the company account, controls billing, invites other users.
Recruiter employer.recruiter
Posts jobs, manages candidate pipelines, coordinates interviews.
Hiring Manager employer.hm
Reviews candidates, gives feedback, approves offers.
Interviewer employer.interviewer
Reads candidate profile, submits interview feedback. Scoped to the interviews they run.
Employer Finance employer.finance
Invoices, subscriptions, cost allocation. No candidate PII.

Partner Portal

4 roles
Partner Owner partner.owner
Creates the partner account, completes KYC/KYB, controls bank details.
Listing Manager partner.listing
Publishes and updates listings or inventory.
Booking / Fulfilment Agent partner.fulfil
Handles bookings, communicates with users, marks services fulfilled.
Partner Finance partner.finance
Views payouts and statements; updates bank details.

Corporate Mobility Portal

4 roles
Corporate Admin corp.admin
Owner of the corporate account. Buys seats, signs contracts, configures global policies.
HR / Mobility Manager corp.mobility
Assigns seats, watches dashboards, handles escalations.
Corporate Finance corp.finance
Invoices, cost-per-relocation reports, policy budgets. No employee PII.
Read-only Viewer corp.viewer
Business-unit leaders or auditors. Aggregate view only.

Admin / Ops Console

5 roles
Super Admin ops.super
Full access. Break-glass role, limited to a handful of staff.
Trust & Verification Officer ops.trust
Reviews KYB, approves or rejects partners, handles SLA breaches and disputes.
Data Curator ops.curator
Maintains the Country Knowledge Graph. Reviews AI-extracted updates. No user PII, no money.
Support Agent ops.support
Handles user tickets. Cannot move money or approve partners.
Finance Ops ops.finance
Escrow exceptions, refunds, payouts, reconciliation.

Expert Portal (optional / premium)

1 role
Verified Expert expert.verified
Licensed external professional (immigration, tax, relocation). Reads consenting users’ plans, provides guidance, gets paid through the platform.

System accounts (not counted in the 22)

2 accounts
Copilot Agent system.copilot
The AI, acting on behalf of a user under explicit consent. Actions are logged and scoped like any other actor.
Integration Service Account system.integration
Tokens for ATS sync, payment webhooks, KYC callbacks, gov portal submissions, etc. Scoped per integration.

06 — Quick referenceWhen the count question comes up again.

The three canonical numbers.
9 personas — Mover, Family, Student, Employer, Partner, HR, Ops, Curator, Expert.
6 portals at launch (plus the optional Expert Portal when the premium tier ships).
22 human roles, spread across portals as 3 · 5 · 4 · 4 · 5 · 1. Plus 2 non-human system accounts.

The rule of thumb. Personas are product language, portals are design language, roles are engineering language. When someone asks “how many X are there?”, clarify which dimension they mean before answering.
