An AGI-native, cross-border life-onboarding platform that guides an individual or family from the decision to move, through securing employment abroad, to arriving and being fully set up in a new country — as one intelligent, execution-enabled system.

01 — OverviewWhat we are building

Glimmora Relocate is not a job board, a relocation agency, or a neobank. It is the intelligence layer that fuses all three into a single guided journey. The AGI Copilot is the user’s front door; everything else — job intelligence, country knowledge, financial modelling, document compliance, marketplace fulfilment, escrowed payments, timeline tracking — is a tool the Copilot orchestrates on the user’s behalf.

The promise

Decision → Stability

From the first thought of moving to being fully settled with a job, home, school, bank, and community — one thread of guidance.

The shape

Intelligence + Execution

Not advice-only. Every recommendation is bookable, payable, trackable, and verifiable through the platform.

The moat

Digital Twin + Flywheel

Each journey updates a knowledge graph + trained twins that make the next user’s plan sharper. Data compounds.

Digital Twins (§9) — why they matter

  • User Twin. Identity, skills, passport, family shape, preferences, consent. The Copilot reasons on the twin, not raw fields.
  • Family Twin. Holds the shape of the family; each member has their own sub-twin with goals (spouse career, child schooling).
  • Job Twin. Projected role: salary, visa tier, location, start date — used to drive Relocation Twin updates.
  • Relocation Twin. The active plan’s state — status, milestones, open tasks, unresolved dependencies, risk score.

11 — Open questionsWhat the SOW does not yet answer

  • Launch corridors. Which origin→destination country pairs do we launch with? The quality of the Country KG is bounded by this choice.
  • Data sourcing for the Country KG. Build it (crawlers + curation), license it (cost-of-living, tax, schools providers), or hybrid?
  • LLM stance. Which provider(s)? Any data-residency constraints? Are we fine-tuning, or pure orchestration with RAG?
  • Escrow regulation. Holding customer funds per country has licensing implications — PSP-as-escrow or formal escrow partner?
  • Identity & KYC. Which KYC vendor(s)? How do we handle variance across jurisdictions?
  • FR coverage. Only FR-1/2/3, FR-40/41, FR-50/51, FR-60/61 are named. We need full FR numbering before planning tickets.
  • Human expert tier. Is the Verified Expert persona in scope for v1, or a later premium add-on?
  • Offline / documents in transit. When a user is mid-move with unreliable connectivity, how much works offline? (Docs, plan, Copilot fallback.)
  • Data retention & right-to-be-forgotten. Twin data is sensitive — exact retention policy + deletion behaviour needs specification.
  • Success metrics. What defines a “completed” relocation journey? We need the metric the flywheel optimises against.
